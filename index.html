Life Sim

Privacy Policy

Your life story stays on your device. We collect only limited diagnostics needed to keep the game reliable.

Who operates Life Sim

Life Sim is operated by NWTC INC ("we", "us", or "our"). This policy applies to the Life Sim Android application with package name app.simlife.

Game data stored on your device

Your character name, country, choices, relationships, simulated finances and health, settings, and saved games are stored locally on your device. We do not receive this game data through normal gameplay.

Diagnostics we may collect

When an unexpected error occurs in a production build, the app may send a limited crash report or diagnostic event. It can include:

  • the app version and operating-system version;
  • device model and basic technical environment;
  • an error message and stack trace;
  • a short technical label describing which app operation failed.

Default personal-information collection is disabled. We do not attach your character, saved game, contacts, precise location, advertising identifier, financial information, or health information to these reports.

Service provider

We use Sentry as a service provider to process crash reports and diagnostics on our behalf. Sentry's privacy information is available at sentry.io/privacy. Diagnostic data may be processed in countries where Sentry or its subprocessors operate.

Why we use diagnostics

We use diagnostics only to investigate crashes, repair defects, protect the app, and improve reliability. We do not use them for advertising, profiling, or selling data.

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you reset the game, clear the app's storage, or uninstall it. Crash diagnostics are retained only as long as needed to investigate reliability issues and are deleted under our Sentry project retention settings.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data and do not share data for advertising. Limited diagnostics are disclosed only to service providers acting on our behalf, or when required by law.

Children

Life Sim is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and rights

You can remove local game data by using the in-app reset control, clearing the app's storage, or uninstalling Life Sim. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights concerning personal data. Because the app has no account system and diagnostics are not linked to an in-app identity, we may need technical details from you to locate a specific report.
