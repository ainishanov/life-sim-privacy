Life Sim

Privacy Policy

Your life story stays on your device. We collect limited usage analytics, diagnostics, and feedback you choose to send so we can improve the game.

Who operates Life Sim

Life Sim is operated by NWTC INC ("we", "us", or "our"). This policy applies to the Life Sim Android and iOS applications with package/bundle identifier app.simlife.

Game data stored on your device

Your character name, relationships, simulated finances and health, story text, settings, and saved games are stored locally on your device. We do not receive the full saved game or the text of your life story. The limited gameplay categories used for analytics are described below.

Usage analytics we may collect

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand whether the game is being installed, opened, and used over time. It may collect:

  • first app opens, sessions, and general engagement;
  • app version, operating-system version, device model, and language;
  • a Firebase installation or app-instance identifier;
  • coarse region information derived by Google from a masked IP address;
  • bounded gameplay events such as starting or ending a life, completing a year, and selecting an event choice or action;
  • technical category values such as event or action ID, module, simulated age and year, selected country pack, locale, and generation number.

We do not send character names, relationship names, story text, saved-game contents, simulated money or health values, contacts, precise location, or files to Firebase Analytics. Android Advertising ID collection and personalized advertising signals are disabled.

Optional rewarded advertising

Some releases include optional rewarded video advertising provided by Google AdMob. You can continue playing without watching an ad. The reward is a benefit inside the game; it is not money and cannot be withdrawn or transferred to another person.

When advertising is enabled and you choose to use it, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share your IP address (which can indicate your approximate location), app- or developer-bounded device identifiers, ad views and interactions, and SDK performance and diagnostic information. Google uses this information for advertising delivery, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention. We do not send your character, full saved game, simulated finances or health, or story text as part of an ad request.

We request non-personalized ads. Android Advertising ID access remains blocked, and we do not request Apple's tracking permission or use IDFA. Other app- or developer-bounded identifiers may still be processed. Ad-related data is encrypted in transit.

We use Google's User Messaging Platform to gather the required advertising choices before requesting ads. Where available, you can revisit those choices through the ad privacy entry on the inheritance screen. Skipping advertising does not prevent ordinary successor continuation. Turning off Firebase usage analytics does not automatically change your separate advertising choices.

Google's advertising data practices and controls are described at policies.google.com/technologies/ads and policies.google.com/privacy. Advertising data is retained under Google's applicable policies and settings.

Diagnostics we may collect

When an unexpected error occurs in a production build, the app may send a limited crash report or diagnostic event. It can include:

  • the app version and operating-system version;
  • device model and basic technical environment;
  • an error message and stack trace;
  • a short technical label describing which app operation failed.

Default personal-information collection is disabled. We do not attach your character, saved game, contacts, precise location, advertising identifier, financial information, or health information to these reports.

Feedback you choose to send

The app includes a private feedback form for suggestions and bug reports. If you use it, we collect:

  • the feedback category you select;
  • the optional text you enter, up to 1,200 characters;
  • the app version, build version, platform, and selected app language;
  • an anonymous Firebase user identifier and the submission time.

The anonymous identifier is created without your name, email address, phone number, or Google account and is used to enforce a one-submission-per-24-hours limit. Feedback is sent privately to the development team and is not posted to Google Play or shown to other players. Please do not include personal information in the optional text.

Security checks

We use Firebase App Check with Google Play Integrity to help distinguish genuine app requests from automated abuse. Google may process device and app integrity signals to provide this security check. We use the result only for fraud prevention, security, and service protection.

Service providers

We use Google Firebase services, including Analytics, Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and App Check, to process usage analytics, receive private feedback, enforce the feedback rate limit, and protect the service. Google's Firebase privacy information is available at firebase.google.com/support/privacy and Google's privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

We use Sentry as a service provider to process crash reports and diagnostics on our behalf. Sentry's privacy information is available at sentry.io/privacy. Analytics, feedback, security, and diagnostic data may be processed in countries where Google, Sentry, or their subprocessors operate.

Why we use this data

We use analytics to count first opens, understand retention, measure which game systems are used, and improve the experience. We use diagnostics to investigate crashes, repair defects, protect the app, and improve reliability. We use submitted feedback to plan, prioritize, and evaluate product improvements. We do not use this data for personalized advertising or sell it.

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you reset the game, clear the app's storage, or uninstall it. Usage analytics and crash diagnostics are retained only as long as needed for product measurement and reliability and are deleted under our Google Analytics and Sentry project retention settings. Private feedback and its anonymous rate-limit record are retained only as long as needed to review suggestions, prevent abuse, improve the game, and meet legal obligations.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data and do not share data for personalized advertising. Limited analytics, feedback, security, and diagnostics are disclosed to our service providers or when required by law. If you choose optional advertising in an ad-enabled release, Google also processes the ad-related data described above for advertising delivery, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention.

Children

Life Sim is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and rights

You can turn off future usage analytics in the app's Privacy settings. You can remove local game data by using the in-app reset control, clearing the app's storage, or uninstalling Life Sim. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights concerning personal data. Because the app has no named account system, we may need technical details such as the approximate submission date, feedback category, and part of the submitted text to locate a feedback record for an access or deletion request.
